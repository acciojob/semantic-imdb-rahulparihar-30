Summary

Interstellar follows Cooper, a former NASA pilot who joins a mission through a wormhole to find a new habitable planet for humanity. The crew faces extreme gravity, time dilation, and difficult choices while trying to save the future of humanity.

Actors

Matthew McConaughey plays Cooper, Anne Hathaway plays Dr. Amelia Brand, and Jessica Chastain plays Murphy Cooper.
